Chargebacks

A chargeback is when a client's bank reverses a card payment. This section explains what happens if the reversed payment has already been paid out to a Stylist.

  • Your chance to respond: When we receive a chargeback on a booking, we will tell the Stylist and ask for any evidence they want us to consider, such as photos of the finished work. Payment providers set short deadlines, so we will give a date by which to reply.
  • When we may recover the amount: We may recover the amount from the Stylist only if the chargeback is decided against HairGo and, after reviewing the evidence, we reasonably conclude that it resulted from the Stylist's failure to provide the service as agreed. Examples include not arriving for the appointment, or providing a service materially different from the one agreed.
  • How much we may recover: We will recover no more than the Stylist's portion of the booking that was paid to them. We will not charge the Stylist the payment provider's chargeback fee.
  • How we recover it: We will first give the Stylist written notice of the booking, the amount and our reasons. The Stylist has 7 days from that notice to ask us to review the decision by contacting us using the details in Section 14. After that period, or after any review that confirms the decision, we may deduct the amount from the Stylist's future payouts. If there are no payouts to deduct it from, the Stylist agrees to repay it within 30 days of our written request.
  • When HairGo carries the loss: We will not recover the amount from a Stylist who provided the service as agreed, including where the chargeback results from a stolen card, other fraud or a false claim by the client.

Nothing in this section limits any right you have under South African law that cannot be excluded.
