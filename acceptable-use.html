Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: October 2026

HairGo connects clients with independent hairstylists who provide services in clients' homes. That only works if everyone can trust and feel safe with each other. This policy sets out the rules for everyone who uses the HairGo mobile application (the "App"), operated by HAIRGO (PTY) LTD (CIPC registration number 2026/762822/07) ("HairGo," "we," "us," or "our"). It forms part of our Terms of Service.

1. Rules for Everyone

  • Be respectful. No harassment, threats, abuse, hate speech or discrimination, whether in person, in chat or in reviews.
  • Keep everyone safe. No violence, intimidation or behaviour that makes the other person feel unsafe. Stylists may leave, and clients may end an appointment, if they feel unsafe; report it to us as soon as you can.
  • No sexual content or advances of any kind towards another user.
  • Be honest. Use your real name and accurate details. Do not impersonate anyone or create accounts for someone else.
  • One account per person. Keep your login private and do not share or sell your account.
  • Keep bookings and payments in the App. Do not use HairGo to find each other and then arrange or pay for bookings outside the App. Off-app arrangements are not protected by HairGo's refund, dispute or safety processes.
  • Respect privacy. Only use another user's address, phone number or photos for the booking they relate to. Do not share them or contact the person for anything unrelated.
  • No illegal activity, including fraud, money laundering, or selling or offering illegal goods or services.
  • Do not misuse the App. No hacking, scraping, automated access, spam, or attempts to get around security or verification.

2. Rules for Clients

  • Give an accurate address and be present (or have an adult present) at the appointment time.
  • Provide a safe, reasonable space for the stylist to work.
  • Pay for confirmed bookings through the App, and do not make false payment disputes or chargebacks.
  • Cancel through the App as early as you can. See our Cancellation & Refund Policy.

3. Rules for Stylists

  • Complete identity verification honestly. Your account must be used only by you.
  • Only show photos of your own work in your profile and portfolio.
  • Offer only services you are qualified and able to perform safely, and quote prices honestly.
  • Arrive on time for confirmed bookings, or cancel through the App as early as possible.
  • Do not ask clients for extra payment outside the App or for their payment details.
  • Use hygienic tools and products, and tell the client in advance about any product they may react to.

4. Reviews

  • Reviews must be honest and based on a real booking.
  • Do not post fake reviews, review your own services, or offer or demand anything in exchange for a review.
  • We may remove reviews that break this policy.

5. Reporting a Problem

If someone breaks this policy, or you feel unsafe, contact us at support@hairgohome.co.za with the booking details. If you are in immediate danger, contact the South African Police Service on 10111 first.

6. What We May Do

If we reasonably believe someone has broken this policy, we may, depending on how serious it is:

  • give a warning;
  • remove content, such as a review or photo;
  • cancel affected bookings;
  • hold payouts while we investigate;
  • suspend or permanently close the account; and
  • report the matter to the police or other authorities where the law requires or allows it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will show the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you in the App.
