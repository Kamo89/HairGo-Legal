Safety first

Verified, protected, supported

✔ Verified stylists

Every stylist completes identity verification against the South African national ID database before they can accept bookings.

🔒 Protected payments

Your payment is held securely after booking and only released to the stylist once the service is completed, with a window to raise any issue.

💬 Dispute support

If something goes wrong, you can raise a dispute. Our team reviews it and funds stay held until it's resolved.
