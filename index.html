Booked, paid and styled, all in the app

From finding a stylist to the moment they knock, every step happens in HairGo.

  1. 1

    Find a pro

    Browse stylists near you by service and area, and look through their work.

  2. 2

    Chat and agree

    Message them and agree on the service, date, time and price.

  3. 3

    Pay in the app

    Pay securely through Paystack. HairGo holds the money until the job is done.

  4. 4

    Get styled

    Follow your stylist's arrival time, then leave a review when you're done.

Safe on both sides of the door

A stranger coming to your home, or going to a client's, should feel safe.

Every stylist is checked

Stylists are reviewed and approved by HairGo before they can take bookings.

Your payment is protected

Money is held after you pay and only released once the job is complete, with time to raise a problem.

Help when something goes wrong

Either side can raise a dispute. We review it, and the money stays held until it's resolved.

For stylists: your business, minus the chasing

No more "I'll pay you Friday." Clients pay up front, people nearby find you, and you set your own prices, area and hours.

  • Clients pay before you arrive
  • Accept or decline every request
  • Your schedule, chats and earnings in one place
  • Paid to your bank account after each job
