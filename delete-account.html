Delete Your HairGo Account

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This page explains how to delete your account in the HairGo app, operated by HAIRGO (PTY) LTD (CIPC registration number 2026/762822/07). It applies to both client and stylist accounts.

Delete your account in the App

  1. Open the HairGo app and log in.
  2. Tap Profile at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  4. Tap Delete to confirm. If you're asked to, enter your password to confirm it's you.

Your account is deleted straight away, and you'll see a message confirming it.

Delete your account by email

If you no longer have the App, or can't log in, email us at support@hairgohome.co.za with the subject "Delete my HairGo account".

  • Send the email from the email address on your HairGo account. If you can't, we'll ask you to confirm a detail only you would know before we delete anything, so that nobody else can delete your account.
  • We'll confirm your request within 2 business days and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your login, so the account can no longer be used.
  • Your personal profile, including your name, contact details and profile photo.
  • Your upcoming bookings and open quotes are cancelled, and the other person is told. If a payment may have been made for a cancelled booking, we review it and arrange any refund due.
  • If you're a client: your name, phone number and service address are removed from the stylist's view of your bookings, and your name is removed from your reviews and from the stylist's chat list.

What we keep, and for how long

Some records have to be kept after an account is deleted, so we can resolve disputes, respond to card chargebacks and meet our legal obligations. Only authorised HairGo administrators can access them.

  • Booking, payment, dispute and job-history records, which can contain names, service addresses, appointment details and related messages or reviews: kept for at least 18 months after the booking.
  • Tax records: generally kept for five years.
  • Company accounting records: kept for about seven years.
  • Records may be kept longer during an audit, investigation or other legal matter.
  • If you're a stylist: your name stays on your clients' past bookings.
  • Chat messages and images you sent stay visible to the person you sent them to.

For more about how we handle your information, see our Privacy Policy.
