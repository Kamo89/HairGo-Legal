Cancellation, Refund & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains how services booked through the HairGo mobile application (the "App") are delivered, what happens when a booking is cancelled, and when you are entitled to a refund. It is operated by HAIRGO (PTY) LTD (CIPC registration number 2026/762822/07) ("HairGo," "we," "us," or "our"). It forms part of our Terms of Service.

All times are measured against the appointment date and time shown on the booking in the App.

1. How Paying for a Booking Works

  • You and the stylist agree on the service in chat, and the stylist sends you a quote. Nothing is charged until you tap Book, and you can simply ignore a quote you don't want.
  • When you book, your saved card is used. A 20% deposit is charged 24 hours before the appointment, or straight away if the appointment is less than 24 hours away.
  • The rest is charged 1 hour after the job is marked complete, unless you report a problem in the App within that hour.
  • If a deposit charged later doesn't go through, we'll ask you to update your card. If it isn't paid 6 hours before the appointment, the booking is cancelled at no charge to you or the stylist.

2. When You (the Client) Cancel a Confirmed Booking

  • More than 24 hours before the appointment: cancelling is free. The deposit hasn't been charged yet, so nothing is taken from your card.
  • 24 hours or less before the appointment: your 20% deposit is kept as the late-cancellation fee, and nothing more is charged. The deposit goes to the stylist for the booking slot they kept open, less any HairGo commission that applies on the date it was charged (0% until 31 December 2026).

The App shows which of these applies before you confirm the cancellation.

3. When the Stylist Cancels

If a stylist cancels a confirmed booking, at any time, you pay nothing. If your deposit was already charged, it is refunded in full.

4. After the Appointment

  • Once a booking has been marked complete, it can no longer be cancelled.
  • If something went wrong with the service, you can report an issue within 1 hour of the booking being marked complete. While an issue is open, the rest of the payment isn't charged until HairGo has reviewed it.
  • HairGo reviews disputes using the booking record, in-app chat and job history, and may decide that you pay the full price, part of it or nothing, and refund your deposit in full or in part, depending on what happened.

5. How Refunds Are Paid

  • Refunds are returned to the payment method you used to pay in the App.
  • Once HairGo processes a refund, the time it takes to reach your account depends on your bank or card provider.
  • Payments made to a stylist outside the App (for example in cash) are not covered by this policy and cannot be refunded by HairGo.

6. How Services Are Delivered

  • HairGo does not ship physical goods. Services are delivered in person by the independent stylist you book, at the South African address you enter on the booking.
  • Your booking in the App shows the agreed service, price, date, time and address. You receive your booking confirmation and updates in the App.
  • The App can show the stylist's estimated arrival time while they travel to you.
  • If your stylist does not arrive, contact us or report the issue in the App. If HairGo confirms the service was not delivered, you receive a full refund.

7. Your Rights Under South African Law

Nothing in this policy limits any right you have under the Consumer Protection Act, 2008 or any other applicable law.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The version in effect when you confirmed your booking applies to that booking. We will show the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you in the App.
