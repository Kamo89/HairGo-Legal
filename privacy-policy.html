Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

HAIRGO (PTY) LTD (CIPC registration number 2026/762822/07), operating as HairGo ("HairGo," "we," "us," or "our"), operates the HairGo mobile application (the "App"), a marketplace connecting clients with independent hairstylists and beauty professionals in South Africa. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

Account Information

  • Full name, email address, and phone number
  • Password (stored securely, encrypted, and never visible to us)
  • Profile photo (optional)

Identity Verification (Stylists Only)

  • South African ID number, full name, and date of birth
  • A photograph of your identity document (South African National ID or Passport)
  • A selfie, used for facial biometric verification — specifically liveness detection and face matching against your identity document
  • This information is submitted to Smile Identity, our identity verification partner, to confirm your identity against official South African records. See Section 5 for details on this third party.

Location Information

  • Client physical addresses, provided when booking a service, so stylists can travel to the correct location
  • Stylist suburb and city, shown on the stylist's profile so clients can find stylists near them. Stylists should not enter a street or home address in these fields.
  • Device location, where permitted by you, to support navigation and address autocomplete features
  • Trip arrival time (stylists only): while a stylist is travelling to a confirmed appointment and has the appointment screen open, the app uses the stylist's device location to estimate their arrival time (ETA). Only the estimated arrival time and trip status are shared with the client; the stylist's location itself is never shared with clients, and location is not collected in the background. You can disable location access in your device settings, though the arrival estimate will not function without it.

Payment Information

  • Payment card details are collected and processed directly by Paystack, our payment processor. HairGo does not store your full card number.
  • If you pay by bank transfer (EFT), you pay from your own bank. We use the amount, payment reference and any details shown on the payment we receive to match it to your booking.
  • Stylist bank account details (bank name, account holder name, account number, account type and branch code), collected to enable payouts for completed bookings

Booking and Usage Information

  • Booking history, appointment details, and service preferences
  • Messages, and any images, exchanged between clients and stylists within the App (including chat images and payment-proof images)
  • Reviews, ratings, and any disputes raised regarding a booking

Device and Technical Information

  • Device push notification tokens, used to send you booking updates and alerts
  • General device and app usage information for troubleshooting and improving the App
  • Crash and error reports, including the device model, operating system, app version, device state at the time of the problem, technical details of the error, an installation identifier, your HairGo account ID and your role (client or stylist). We do not deliberately add your name, email address or phone number to these reports.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Facilitate bookings between clients and stylists
  • Verify stylist identities to help maintain a trustworthy platform
  • Process payments and stylist payouts
  • Share a stylist's live arrival time with the client during an active trip
  • Send booking confirmations, updates, and important notifications
  • Provide customer support
  • Investigate and resolve disputes between clients and stylists
  • Detect, diagnose and fix crashes and errors
  • Improve and maintain the App's functionality and security
  • Keep a record of booking activity to help resolve disputes and respond to payment chargebacks
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Who We Share Information With

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as necessary to operate the App:

  • Stylists you book: When you book a service, your name, service address and phone number are shared with that stylist so they can travel to you and contact you about the appointment.
  • Clients using the App: Stylist profiles show clients the stylist's name, photo, services, about text, ratings and reviews, online status, and suburb and city. A stylist's phone number and email address are kept in a private record that other users cannot access. Clients and stylists can message each other in the in-app chat.
  • Smile Identity: Stylist identity details — including the ID number, name, identity document photo, and verification selfie — are shared with Smile Identity to perform identity verification, facial biometric matching, and liveness checks.
  • Paystack: Payment information is shared with Paystack to process transactions securely.
  • Service providers: We use Firebase (Google) for authentication, data storage, push notifications and crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics), and Expo to deliver push notifications.
  • Google Places: Address text you type, and the place you select, are sent to Google to suggest and look up addresses.
  • Route estimates: While a stylist is travelling to an appointment, the stylist's current location and the appointment location are sent as map coordinates to the OSRM routing service (router.project-osrm.org) to estimate distance and arrival time.
  • UI Avatars: A stylist's name may be sent to ui-avatars.com to create a placeholder profile image in a client's bookings.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information where required by law or to protect the safety of our users.

4. Data Storage and International Transfers

Your information is stored using Firebase, a service provided by Google, which may process and store data outside of South Africa. The other service providers named in Section 3 may also process data outside South Africa. By using the App, you acknowledge that your information may be transferred to and processed in countries outside South Africa, where data protection laws may differ. We take reasonable steps to ensure your information remains protected in accordance with this Policy and applicable law, including South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

5. Identity Verification Details

To help ensure the safety of our platform, stylists are required to complete an identity verification check through Smile Identity, a third-party identity verification provider. This check confirms that the ID number and personal details you provide match official South African records. It also includes submitting a photograph of your identity document and a selfie, which Smile Identity uses to perform facial biometric matching and liveness detection to confirm you are the genuine holder of the document. This biometric processing is used solely for identity verification and fraud prevention. Smile Identity's own privacy practices govern how they handle the information submitted to them for this check.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide you services, comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. If you close your account, we may retain certain information as required by law or for legitimate business purposes, such as fraud prevention and financial record-keeping. This includes booking, payment, dispute and job-history records, which can contain names, service addresses, appointment details and related messages or reviews. We keep these records for at least 18 months after the booking so we can resolve disputes and respond to chargebacks, and longer where the law requires it. For example, tax records are generally kept for five years and company accounting records for about seven years, or longer during an audit, investigation or other legal matter. Funds for a booking are held from payment until three hours after the stylist marks the appointment complete (the dispute window), after which, absent a dispute, payout may be released.

7. Your Rights and Account Deletion

Under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information, subject to legal and legitimate business retention requirements
  • Object to certain processing of your information
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

You can delete your account directly in the App at any time under Profile → Delete account. This removes your login and clears your personal profile. Your upcoming bookings and open quotes are cancelled and the other person is told. If a payment may have been made for a cancelled booking, we review it and arrange any refund due. If you are a client, your name, phone number and service address are removed from the stylist's view of your bookings, and your name is removed from your reviews and from the stylist's chat list. If you are a stylist, your name stays on your clients' past bookings.

Deleting your account does not remove the booking, payment, dispute and job-history records described in Section 6, which only authorised HairGo administrators can continue to access for those purposes. Messages and images you sent in chat also stay visible to the person you sent them to. You may also exercise any of these rights by contacting us using the details below.

If you believe we have not handled your personal information properly, you may also lodge a complaint with South Africa's Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za).

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) to our service providers, and payment secrets are handled server-side and never stored in the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected such information, we will take steps to delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page, and where appropriate, through the App.
