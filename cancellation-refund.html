Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what happens when a booking made through the HairGo mobile application (the "App") is cancelled, and when you are entitled to a refund. It is operated by HAIRGO (PTY) LTD (CIPC registration number 2026/762822/07) ("HairGo," "we," "us," or "our"). It forms part of our Terms of Service.

All times are measured against the appointment date and time shown on the booking in the App.

1. Before the Stylist Confirms

  • You can withdraw a booking request at any time before the stylist confirms it, at no charge.
  • If the stylist does not respond within 24 hours, the request expires automatically and nothing is charged.
  • If the stylist proposes a different price and you decline it, the booking is cancelled at no charge.

2. When You (the Client) Cancel a Confirmed Booking

  • More than 24 hours before the appointment: you receive a full refund.
  • 24 hours or less before the appointment: you receive an 80% refund. The remaining 20% is a late-cancellation fee: 10% is paid to the stylist for the booking slot they kept open, and 10% is retained by HairGo.

The App shows which of these applies before you confirm the cancellation.

3. When the Stylist Cancels

If a stylist cancels a confirmed booking, at any time, you receive a full refund.

4. After the Appointment

  • Once a booking has been marked complete, it can no longer be cancelled.
  • If something went wrong with the service, you can report an issue within 3 hours of the booking being marked complete. While an issue is open, the stylist's payment is held until HairGo has reviewed it.
  • HairGo reviews disputes using the booking record, in-app chat and job history, and may decide on a full refund, a partial refund or no refund depending on what happened.

5. How Refunds Are Paid

  • Refunds are returned to the payment method you used to pay in the App.
  • Once HairGo processes a refund, the time it takes to reach your account depends on your bank or card provider.
  • Payments made to a stylist outside the App (for example in cash) are not covered by this policy and cannot be refunded by HairGo.

6. Your Rights Under South African Law

Nothing in this policy limits any right you have under the Consumer Protection Act, 2008 or any other applicable law.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The version in effect when you confirmed your booking applies to that booking. We will show the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you in the App.
